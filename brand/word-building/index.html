Letter Workshop: art direction preview

Static prototype for approval. Nothing here is the final game. It shows the cozy craft room, the four friends, Hazel as the shop owner, the letter tiles and slots, and the word wall for Word Building for Grade 1. Same bones as the garden and the pond (rounded shapes, one outline weight, ink on light, teal for UI), in a third mood: indoors, warm wood, paper and lamplight. Gentle idle motion only. Scenes are 960 x 600. The picture on the easel is a placeholder; the 36 word pictures are drawn separately.

1. The workshop at the start of a round

The mouse holds up a picture card, Hazel points at the easel, the word is four letters so there are four slots, and the first slot glows (the "Letter" hint). The tray holds the letters plus distractors.

Wide screen check: the same scene on a 1600 wide canvas. Wall, floor, paper and bench continue past the 960 scene edges with no seam.

2. Mid word

Two letters are seated, a third tile is lifted with a soft shadow, and one tile in the tray wobbles (wrong letter, no red mark). The hedgehog waves and Hazel is neutral.

3. Word complete: the letters light up

All four tiles are lit (gold halo, green rim). The bunny hops, Hazel cheers, and a new card joins the word wall with sparkles.

4. A five letter word

Level 5 uses five slots and a longer tray. Three letters are seated and the next slot glows. The robin waits.

5. Letter tiles

Tiles are 80 x 80 (never smaller than 64 at display size), a rounded paper-maple block with a solid darker edge like our pressable buttons. Letters are ink, lowercase, with single-story a and g.

Letters are always ink (#3B2A20) on a light tile face (#FFF3D6, #FFF1A8 when lit): contrast about 12:1 and 11:1. The lit state never relies on color alone: it adds a gold halo, a thicker green rim and sparkles. Wobble is a soft orange rim plus motion arcs, never red.

Hand-off to the game skin

Everything above is in scene.ts as plain strings (generated by build.py, so this page and the module cannot drift). Scene layers (backdrop, lamp, wallShelf, craftShelf, bench, tray, easelFrame) are <g> fragments for a 960 x 600 scene, each with its own gradients. Sprites (hazel, cast, tileSvg, slotSvg, pictureCard, wallCard) are standalone <svg> strings with headroom in the viewBox; SPRITE_VB lists each viewBox and anchor. Letters are stroked paths (single-story a and g), so the tiles need no font and render the same everywhere.

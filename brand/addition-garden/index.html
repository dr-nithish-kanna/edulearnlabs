Addition Garden: art direction preview

Static prototype for approval. Nothing here is the final game. It shows the look, the cast and the garden reward so we can agree on the art before any development. Gentle idle motion only (bobbing, glowing count badge). It follows the v3 style guide: cream and teal, Fredoka numbers, rounded shapes, soft tinted shadows, no red in feedback.

1. Seed Stall: filling the basket (levels 1 to 3)

The ladybug orders 3 daisies and 4 tulips. The child drags flowers from the two bins into the basket. The yellow badge counts as they go. Hazel is the shopkeeper. Progress dots on top, My garden shelf at the bottom.

2. The "How many?" step

After both groups are in, they merge into a bouquet and the child taps the total. This is the step that makes them add. A wrong tap dims that choice (here the 6) and never shows the answer.

3. The customers

The ladybug is the only coral red thing in the game. It is a character color, not feedback, so it does not break the "no red in feedback" rule. Say if you want a different color.

4. My garden: seed, sprout, bloom

Each solved order plants one seed. Plants grow as the child plays more sessions, never with time away. Six species, in a fixed cycle that matches the bin flowers:

SunflowerTulipDaisyLavenderStrawberrySmall tree

Only the daisy and the tulip are drawn in this preview. The other four would follow the same simple, rounded style.

Quilt Picnic: art direction preview

Static prototype for approval. Nothing here is the final game. It shows a cozy afternoon meadow with a patchwork quilt, the board of fabric flaps, the five flap states, sixteen friend pictures, the "Your Quilt" reward strip and the quiet way a mismatch closes. Hazel is the picnic host. Same bones as the garden, the pond and the workshop (rounded shapes, one outline weight, ink on light, teal for UI), in a fourth mood: golden afternoon, apricot sky, mauve hills and olive grass. Scenes are 960 x 600. Everything on this page is a local file with no external requests, so headings use a system rounded font here (the real game ships subset Fredoka and Andika).

1. The meadow mid-game (3 x 4 board, level 4)

Four flaps are matched (two ladybugs, two bees, glowing with the friend hopping), one flap is up on its own (the fox), one closed flap twinkles as the sparkle hint, and six are still closed. The voice says "Find two that match", shown here as Hazel's bubble. The back button and audio toggle sit in the top corners.

1b. Same scene on a wide screen (1600 x 600 window)

The sky, hills, grass, clouds, trees and flowers are drawn thousands of units past the 960 x 600 box, and every gradient is in userSpaceOnUse. A wide tablet or a desktop sees more meadow and no seam. The quilt, board and Hazel stay in the 960 box.

2. Flap states

The flap is one rounded fabric square. Closed: stitched border, a button, and a fabric pattern (dots, stripes, checks) so the board looks like a real patchwork. The six fabric colours rotate so no two neighbours match, which is a handy landmark for remembering where things are. Lifting folds the flap up and back (a small sliver stays above the pocket), so a 3D card flip is not needed.

3. Friends (16 pictures)

Hand-drawn SVG, one 3 px ink outline, flat fills, big eyes and cheeks where there is a face. Each viewBox is 100 x 100 and the art stays inside 8 to 92, so ears, antennae, spikes and stems keep headroom. Ladybug, snail, bee and rabbit link to earlier games. These ids become the game's item ids.

Small size check (48 px and 32 px)

Headroom check (dashed blue line is the 6 to 94 safe box)

4. Your Quilt (reward strip, owned by the website)

One patch is sewn for each finished board and stored as facts on the child's profile. It appears on the website and at the start and end of a session. Nothing is lost, nothing resets, no streak. Unsewn slots are faded outlines.

5. A mismatch is quiet

Two different friends: both flaps stay up for a short moment, then fold back down slowly. No buzzer, no red, no cross, no shake, no counter. The child is never told they missed. The last card is a live loop of the closing.

6. Board sizes (the whole scene, scaled down)

The board area is fixed and the cell size is the biggest square that fits. At 960 x 600: 2 x 2 cells are 150 px (capped), 3 x 4 cells are 116 px, 4 x 4 cells are about 86 px. One flap is lifted in each so the picture size can be judged.

Distinct from the earlier games: the garden is sunny blue and bright green, the pond is dusk navy and purple, the workshop is warm wood and paper. Quilt Picnic is apricot, mauve and olive with fabric colours (berry, mustard, sage, lavender, terracotta, teal). No red is used for feedback. The match glow is warm gold.

Motion uses transform and opacity only: soft glow pulse, sparkle twinkle, the matched friend hop, slow clouds, Hazel's idle bob, blink and a small wave, and the gentle flap closing. With prefers-reduced-motion every loop stops and each state keeps a still version (glow stays lit, sparkles stay visible, the flap closing demo shows the closed state).

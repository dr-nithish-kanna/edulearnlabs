Pond Hop: art direction preview

Static prototype for approval. Nothing here is the final game. It shows the dusk pond look, the cast and the glowing sky reward for Subtraction for Grade 1, so we can agree on the art before any development. Same bones as Addition Garden (rounded shapes, one outline weight, Fredoka numbers, teal buttons) in a different mood: evening, lantern light, things leaving instead of arriving. Gentle idle motion only. All scenes are 960 x 600.

1. Pad scene (levels 1 to 3)

The duckling asks for 3 frogs to go for a swim. Eight frogs sit on the lily pad, the dashed patch of water is where they go. Hazel is the lantern keeper on the far bank.

Wide screen check: the same scene on a 1620 wide canvas. The sky, hills, water and reeds continue past the 960 scene edges with no seam.

2. Same scene, mid-action

One frog is lifted with a soft shadow, one leaps over the water above a ripple ring, one already swims. Five frogs are left on the pad. The bubble shows the order with the frogs still to send dimmed.

3. The "How many are still on the pad?" step

The pad calms down with the 5 remaining frogs. Three big choices, each 176 by 96 px: an off by one answer, the right answer and the wrong operation (8 + 3).

4. Lantern scene (levels 4 and 5)

Thirteen fireflies: a full ten-frame, plus three that were in the second frame and have flown out into the night sky (their empty cells show a dim ghost). The card at the bottom left shows the "10" token idea for level 5: a full frame folds into one glowing 10.

5. The cast

Same line weight, cheeks and eyes as the garden cast. Every character has headroom in its box so ears, eyestalks and antennae never clip.

The four friends (anonymous, each one asks one order)

Frog (the item the child moves), four poses

Firefly (the item in the lantern), lit and dim

6. End screen: the pond is glowing

Ten fireflies, one per solved order, drift together toward the lantern. Nothing is saved to the website and nothing is lost if the child stops. Stars still go to the website as today.

Symbols to import into the game skin

ell-pond-backdrop (pad levels) and ell-pond-backdrop-night (lantern levels), both 960 x 600 with overflow visible and userSpaceOnUse gradients, ell-pond-bank, ell-pond-pad, ell-pond-frog, ell-pond-frog-lifted, ell-pond-frog-leap, ell-pond-frog-swim, ell-pond-ripple, ell-pond-firefly, ell-pond-firefly-dim, ell-pond-lantern (hand lantern), ell-pond-jar (big glass jar, frames are drawn by the game), ell-pond-star, ell-pond-duckling, ell-pond-turtle, ell-pond-dragonfly, ell-pond-fish (each with a -happy twin), and the Hazel poses ell-pond-hazel, -keeper, -cheer, -point (preview only: the game uses the kit's Hazel). Gradients and filters used by the symbols: ell-pond-sky, -water, -sky-night, -water-night, -wood, -glow, -glow-warm, -soft. Port like the garden: one string per symbol, items map frog: 'ell-pond-frog', firefly: 'ell-pond-firefly', customers list for the four friends, backdrop per layout.

Open questions for review: (1) the duckling, turtle, dragonfly and fish are all friendly and bright; the dragonfly is the only pink one, as the ladybug was the only red one in the garden. (2) Frogs are lime green on a teal-green pad: they separate by value and outline, not by hue alone. (3) The preview uses Fredoka and Andika from Google Fonts. Offline, the page falls back to a rounded system font.

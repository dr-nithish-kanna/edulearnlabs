Bead Parlor

Art preview for Color pattern repeat (Kindergarten, logic). Status: for owner approval. Scene is 960 x 600, same as the other games. Beads, cast and layout are a proposal, nothing is built yet.

1. Playing: continue the pattern (level 2)

The spider hangs from a thread beside the "My bracelets" board. The string has one dashed gap at the end, and it pulses softly (that is hint 2, "Gap"). The tray holds the right bead plus spares. Hazel waits at the top right as the host and hint button.

2. Level 1: copy the pattern

The card above is the pattern to copy. The child builds it below, left to right. It is the easiest level, so it never counts as "reading" a pattern, only as matching one.

3. Done: the bracelet is tied

Each bead lights left to right with its note, so the pattern plays as a tune. The bracelet is tied with a bow and flies to the "My bracelets" shelf (website-owned reward). In reduced motion the lift and fly become a fade.

4. The six beads: color and shape

Every bead has its own color and its own shape. The pattern can be read by shape alone. Levels 1 to 3 use only red circle, blue square and yellow triangle.

5. Color-blind check (grayscale)

Same beads with all color removed. If you can still tell all six apart, a child who cannot see the colors can still solve the game. Red and green are the usual trouble pair, so they are never both in levels 1 to 3.

6. What I need you to check

  • The spider. Friendly or a bit much for 5 year olds? Some children fear spiders. The alternative is a caterpillar or a ladybug stringing the beads. I drew a big-eyed, smiling, plum spider on purpose, and it is your call.
  • The six beads. Shapes read clearly in grayscale? Any shape you want swapped?
  • The craft room. Peach wall, wood floor, window, hanging yarn garland. New look compared to the garden (sunny), pond (dusk), workshop (wood) and meadow (picnic).
  • Layout. Bead tiles are 104 px in the 960 x 600 scene (65 px on a 667 x 375 phone), seven beads at most per string.

Sound and motion are not shown in a static page. Motion is transform and opacity only, with a reduced-motion version of every animation. Fonts fall back to system fonts here, the game ships Fredoka and Andika.
